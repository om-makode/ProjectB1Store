Privacy policy

NearKart ("we") is operated by [FILL: legal business name], [FILL: address]. This policy explains what personal data the NearKart app and website collect and why, as required by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address and phone number.
  • Delivery addresses: the addresses you save, with their map location.
  • Orders: what you ordered, when, prices paid, and any issue you report (including photos you attach).
  • Device: a push-notification token if you allow notifications, and basic crash information.
  • Location: only when you tap "Use current location", to check delivery and fill your address. We never track location in the background.
  • Microphone: only while you use voice search; speech is converted to text on your phone.

Why we use it

To deliver your orders, contact you about them, prevent misuse, comply with tax and consumer law, and (only if you allow it) send offers. We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

Our store staff (to pack and deliver), and service providers that run the app for us: Supabase (database and login hosting), Expo/Google Firebase (push notifications) and, if enabled, our payment and crash-reporting providers. They may process data only on our instructions.

How long we keep it

Account data until you delete your account. Order and invoice records for as long as tax law requires (typically 8 years), with your personal details removed after account deletion.

Your rights

You can see and correct your details in the app (Account > Profile), delete your account at any time (Account > Profile > Delete account), turn off offers (Account > Notification settings), or write to our grievance officer (see Contact).
