Cancellation & refunds

Cancelling an order

You can cancel from the order page in the app until the store starts packing it. After that, call the store; we'll help if we can.

Missing, damaged or wrong items

Report it from the order page (Report an issue) within 48 hours of delivery, ideally with a photo. Depending on the case we will replace the item, refund it, or adjust the amount you pay.

Items we couldn't deliver

If an item is unavailable while packing, it is removed and your total goes down. You are told in the app before delivery.

Refunds

For cash-on-delivery orders, refunds are given as cash on your next delivery or by UPI transfer, within 7 days of approval. For online payments (when available), refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days of approval.

Not returnable

Perishable items (fruits, vegetables, dairy, bakery) can't be returned once accepted in good condition, except for quality issues reported within 48 hours.
